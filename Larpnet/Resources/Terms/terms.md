# Terms of Use

Larpnet is a community for people involved in larps. To keep it usable and safe, everyone who posts, follows, or messages on Larpnet agrees to the following.

## No tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users

Larpnet has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behavior of any kind, including harassment, hate speech, threats, sexually explicit material involving minors, and content that promotes violence. Accounts and content that violate this policy will be removed.

## Reporting and blocking

Every post and profile has a way to report it to moderators or block the account, right from that post or profile's "•••" menu. Reports are reviewed by the Larpnet server's moderators, who can remove content and suspend or ban the accounts responsible.

## Your account

You're responsible for what you post under your account. You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Continuing past this screen means you agree to these terms and to Larpnet's server acting on reports as described above.
